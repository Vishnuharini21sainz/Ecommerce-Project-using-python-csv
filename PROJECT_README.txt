# E-Commerce Product Analysis

## Project Title
E-Commerce Product Sales and Customer Rating Analysis Using Python

## Tools
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Objectives
1. Analyze product categories and product distribution.
2. Study pricing and discount patterns.
3. Identify top-selling products.
4. Identify highest-revenue products.
5. Analyze customer ratings and reviews.
6. Study relationships between price, discount, ratings, and sales.
7. Compare category-wise revenue.
8. Compare average price and rating across categories.
9. Identify products with low stock and high sales.
10. Visualize insights using Matplotlib and Seaborn.

## Dataset
100 e-commerce products with 11 columns:
Product_ID, Product_Name, Category, Price, Discount_Percent, Final_Price,
Rating, Reviews, Units_Sold, Revenue, Stock

## 10 Analysis Questions
1. Which category has the highest number of products?
2. Which category generates the highest total revenue?
3. Which are the top 10 products by units sold?
4. Which products have the highest ratings?
5. Is there a relationship between price and rating?
6. Does discount affect units sold?
7. Which category has the highest average rating?
8. Which category has the highest average price?
9. Which products generate the highest revenue?
10. Which products have low stock and high sales?

## 10 Graphs
1. Number of Products by Category
2. Total Revenue by Category
3. Top 10 Products by Units Sold
4. Distribution of Product Ratings
5. Price vs Product Rating
6. Discount Percentage vs Units Sold
7. Average Product Rating by Category
8. Average Product Price by Category
9. Top 10 Products by Revenue
10. Correlation Heatmap

## How to Run
Install libraries:
pip install pandas matplotlib seaborn

Keep the CSV and Python file in the same folder, then run:
python ecommerce_product_analysis.py
